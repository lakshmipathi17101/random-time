### Description (≤ 4000 chars)
```
RandomTime takes the stress out of scheduling by generating a perfectly random time within any range you choose — then turning that time into a calendar event with smart reminders.

Whether you need to schedule a workout, a break, a task, or any activity you've been putting off, RandomTime picks the time for you. Just set a range (e.g. 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM), tap Generate, and let randomness do the work.

KEY FEATURES

• Random Time Generator — Instantly generate 1, 3, or 5 random times within your chosen range. Switch between 12-hour and 24-hour formats.

• Task Scheduling — Turn any generated time into a saved task with a title, date, category, priority, and optional notes.

• Smart Notifications — Get a reminder before your task and an alarm at the exact moment. Choose from preset times or set a custom reminder interval.

• Notification Actions — Mark a task Done or Postpone it to a new random time directly from the notification tray — without opening the app.

• Task Management — Search, filter, sort, and bulk-manage all your tasks. Mark tasks done, edit them, postpone them, or share them.

• Statistics — See your total tasks, completion rate, and productivity at a glance.

• Privacy First — All data is stored locally on your device. No account required. No data sent to any server.

PERFECT FOR

- People who procrastinate — let randomness break decision paralysis
- Habit builders — schedule healthy breaks, stretches, or hydration reminders
- Productivity lovers — randomize your task schedule to keep things fresh
- Anyone who wants a simple, beautiful task scheduler

RandomTime is fast, lightweight, and works entirely offline.
```

### Full Description (≤ 4000 chars)
```
RandomTime takes the guesswork out of scheduling — and makes randomness your productivity superpower.

Set a time range, tap Generate, and RandomTime picks a random time within it. Then turn that time into a saved task with a title, date, category, and priority. Smart notifications remind you before it starts, and an alarm fires at the exact moment.

FEATURES

🎲 RANDOM TIME GENERATOR
Generate 1, 3, or 5 random times in one tap. Set any from/to range. Switch between 12h and 24h display.

📅 CALENDAR TASKS
Schedule tasks at your generated time. Set a date, add notes, choose a category (Work, Personal, Health, Other) and priority (High, Medium, Low).

🔔 SMART NOTIFICATIONS
Get a reminder X minutes before your task — and an alarm at the exact time. Choose preset reminders (5, 10, 15, 30 min) or set a custom interval.

📲 NOTIFICATION ACTIONS
Mark Done or Postpone your task from the notification itself. Postpone reschedules to a fresh random time.

📋 FULL TASK MANAGEMENT
Search tasks, filter by status (Pending/Done), sort by time/priority/creation, and bulk delete. Share any task via the system share sheet.

📊 STATISTICS
Track total tasks, done count, and completion percentage at a glance.

🔒 100% PRIVATE
No account. No cloud. No tracking. All data stored locally using SQLite.

WHO IT'S FOR

→ Procrastinators: let randomness break the "when should I do this?" loop
→ Habit builders: set a random break, walk, or hydration reminder each day
→ Productivity enthusiasts: beat decision fatigue with a truly random schedule
→ Students & professionals: schedule tasks without overthinking

Lightweight, offline, and battery-friendly. No ads, no subscriptions.
```
