## Full description

RandomTime helps you stop overthinking and start doing. Generate a random time for your task, get a gentle nudge when it arrives, and build a streak — even on off days.

**Key features**

- **Random time generator** — set a range (e.g. 9am–5pm), tap Generate, get a random time. Skip lunch, sleep, and off-hours automatically.
- **Task nudges** — save the generated time as a task. A pre-nudge reminder arrives a few minutes before so you can wrap up what you're doing.
- **Overlay alarm** — when task time hits, a full-screen overlay fires even if your phone is locked. No more silently missed reminders.
- **Surprise Me** — tap once and RandomTime scatters your pending tasks across the day using your energy level as a guide.
- **Streaks that heal** — one grace day per week so a missed day doesn't wipe your progress.
- **Progress dashboard** — see completions, streaks, and category breakdowns at a glance.
- **Done from tray** — mark tasks done, postpone, or re-roll directly from the notification without opening the app.

Built for ADHD brains: no complex setup, no subscriptions, works offline.
