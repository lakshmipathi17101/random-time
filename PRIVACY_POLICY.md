# Privacy Policy for RandomTime

**Last updated: April 7, 2026**

## Overview

RandomTime ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal task scheduler that helps you generate random times for tasks and set reminders. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is handled.

## Data We Collect

### Data Stored on Your Device Only

All data created by RandomTime is stored **exclusively on your device** using a local SQLite database. This includes:

- Task titles and notes
- Scheduled event dates and times
- Task categories, priorities, and recurrence settings
- App settings (theme preference, time format, time window)

**We do not collect, transmit, or store any of this data on external servers.**

### Device Permissions We Request

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Calendar (Read & Write)** | To add your scheduled tasks to your device calendar app. We only create and manage events created by RandomTime. |
| **Notifications** | To send you reminders and alarms at the scheduled times you set. |
| **Boot Completed** | To restore scheduled notifications after your device restarts. |

We request these permissions only when you use the relevant features. You may deny these permissions — the app will still function for task management without notifications or calendar integration.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect personal identifiers (name, email, phone number).
- We do not collect location data.
- We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics trackers.
- We do not share any data with third parties.
- We do not transmit any data over the internet.

## Calendar Access

When you save a task with "Add to Calendar" enabled, RandomTime requests write access to your device calendar. We:

- Create calendar events only for tasks you explicitly save
- Read your existing calendars only to find a suitable writable calendar to add events to
- Never modify or delete calendar events that were not created by RandomTime
- Never read the content of your existing calendar events

## Notifications

RandomTime uses local notifications (scheduled on-device). No push notification service or third-party notification provider is used. Notification data never leaves your device.

## Data Retention and Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all data at any time by:

1. Deleting individual tasks within the app
2. Clearing the app's storage in your device Settings → Apps → RandomTime → Storage → Clear Data
3. Uninstalling the app

## Children's Privacy

RandomTime does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app contains no user accounts, no online features, and no data collection mechanisms.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
